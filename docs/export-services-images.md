# Export services imagery

Created on 2026-08-28 using the **built-in imagegen tool**, not the CLI fallback. These are illustrative placeholders, not photographs of the company's actual ships, premises, products or personnel. Selected originals remain in the generation folders; optimized WebP copies are saved in `public/assets/` and consumed by `/export-services`. The only postprocessing was resizing and WebP compression, with no compositing or content changes.

## New AI assets and final prompts

### `public/assets/export-services-hero.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide landscape hero photograph for a premium export and logistics services website
Primary request: A photorealistic aerial drone photograph of one large cargo ship carrying neat multicolored shipping containers through deep teal ocean.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape composition, approximately 16:9. The entire ship is positioned in the RIGHT half of the image, its bow pointing toward the lower left and its stern toward the upper right. A realistic white foamy trailing wake extends from the stern toward the upper right. Keep the LEFT 45 percent as calm open ocean in darker teal tones with clear uninterrupted negative space for a white HTML headline that will be added separately. The photograph fills the frame; no horizon, coast, or land.
Lighting/mood: Natural daylight with subtle sunlit sea texture, believable marine colors, polished editorial photography.
Materials/textures: Realistic hull, orderly stacks of shipping containers, finely detailed rippling water and natural foam.
Constraints: Generate a new photograph, not a screenshot or web mockup. No text, lettering, brand logos, watermarks, graphic lines, interfaces, or added typography.
